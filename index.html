Getting There

Flights, Transit & Parking

Dallas is served by two convenient airports. Plan your arrival early — members are encouraged to be in Dallas by 5:00 PM CT on Wednesday, July 29 for General Session I and the Opening Reception.

✈️

Airports

DFW DAL

Two airports serve the convention. Dallas/Fort Worth International (DFW) is the primary hub with the most connections. Dallas Love Field (DAL) is a closer, smaller option — ideal for Southwest travelers.

Both airports are eligible for chapter reimbursement. Book early for the best fares.

🚕

DART directions from DFW: Take the Orange Line from the airport to West End Station, then transfer to the Red or Blue Line to Union Station — connected to the hotel via an underground concourse.
